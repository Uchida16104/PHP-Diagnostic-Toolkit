Evidence standard: mark a root cause as confirmed only after you can reproduce the failure, identify the causal change, and demonstrate that the targeted fix removes it.

A verified fix reproduces the original failure before the change, removes it after the targeted change, and passes a regression test on the relevant PHP version and dependencies. A disappearing error alone may be a workaround rather than a confirmed cause.

05 / Repeat

Follow a root-cause workflow

Keep the investigation reproducible and distinguish observed facts from assumptions.

Diagnostic workflow

Adjust the steps to your team's incident process.

Mermaid
flowchart TD A[Record exact symptom and environment] --> B[Create a minimal reproduction] B --> C{Can the failure be reproduced?} C -- No --> D[Collect logs and compare environments] D --> B C -- Yes --> E[Form a testable cause hypothesis] E --> F[Change one relevant variable] F --> G{Does the result support the hypothesis?} G -- No --> E G -- Yes --> H[Apply targeted fix] H --> I[Run regression tests on target PHP version] I --> J[Document cause, evidence, and limits]
Definition of done: the failure can be reproduced, a causal condition has been demonstrated, a targeted change resolves it, and a regression test guards against recurrence.
